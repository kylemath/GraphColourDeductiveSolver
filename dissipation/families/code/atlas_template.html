Rule by rule: which degree pays which

Each arrow is a set of rule parts, from the sending vertex's degree (left) to the degree condition on the receiving neighbour (right), mirror pairs counted once. Width = number of parts. Tunnels are drawn as one arrow from the neighbour that pays to the neighbour that receives, both degree conditions shown.

On the whole world: average charge moved per adjacent pair

For every pair of adjacent degrees (a, b), the average charge in 1/60 that a pays b through the rules, over every neighbourhood with a receiver of degree 5 to 11 (5 to 8 exhaustive, 9 to 11 sampled; unknown outer degrees uniform). Width is on a log scale, so the rare push and tunnel parts stay visible.
