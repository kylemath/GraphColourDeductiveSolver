# Graph fixtures and a separated-curvature research direction

The stdlib builder `build.py` produces F32 (pentakis dodecahedron, dual to truncated icosahedron) and F42 (frequency-two triangular subdivision of the icosahedron). Explicit oriented triangles reconstruct their rotation systems. Counts are respectively (32,90,60) and (42,120,80), with twelve degree-five vertices and every other vertex degree six. All degree-five vertices have only degree-six neighbours.

The JSON files include one explicit adjacency/rotation-preserving permutation from root 0 to each degree-five root. These are obtained by propagating the base icosahedron's oriented dart maps and lifting to face-centres or edge-midpoints; each lifted permutation is checked directly. Transitivity is therefore demonstrated by permutations, not inferred from a group order.

GF(2) computations give incidence ranks 31/41 and triangular-boundary ranks 59/79, exactly the cycle-space dimensions. Every triangular boundary has even incidence at every vertex. Thus the computed boundary image equals the cycle kernel; this is a finite filling certificate, not a Lean theorem. Connectedness, dart-orbit triangle lengths, simplicity, Euler characteristic, and degrees are also checked. No colourings or WP12 producer experiments were run.

## Equivariance and its limitation

A graph isomorphism transports a colouring by c'(p(v))=c(v), carries the root boundary onto the new root boundary, and maps each bichromatic component bijectively to the corresponding component. Whole-component swaps commute with this transport. Component sizes, exterior sizes, adjacency, boundary links, singleton/repeated-colour roles and degree tests are preserved. Hence aggregate mass and the declared structural features are preserved, provided their definitions contain no smallest-label choice. Global colour permutation likewise merely permutes pair indices. Ordered neighbour tuples can acquire a cyclic shift; aggregate scores are unchanged, but a first-appearance canonical representation must actually re-canonicalize after transport. Label-dependent move ordering is not equivariant and must not be claimed to be. These are mathematical arguments about the named definitions, not machine-checked equivariance theorems.

## The more useful infinite family: long fullerene cylinders

Duality makes a degree-five vertex a pentagonal fullerene face; a degree-six vertex becomes a hexagon. Euler's twelve units of curvature constrain the number of defects, not the number of hexagons or the length of corridors between defects. Root symmetry therefore cannot bound the amount of interior structure.

An explicit capped-cylinder triangulation has two hubs and m pentagonal rings, m >= 2. Within each ring, join cyclic neighbours. Between successive rings, join (i,j) to (i+1,j) and (i+1,j-1), indices modulo 5. Join each hub to its end ring. It has 5m+2 vertices: the hubs and ten end-ring vertices have degree five, all other vertices degree six. The annulus has triangles (i,j),(i,j+1),(i+1,j) and (i,j),(i+1,j),(i+1,j-1); cap triangles complete the sphere. For m=2 this is an icosahedron. As m grows, the twelve curvature defects remain fixed while the central hexagonal corridor grows arbitrarily long.

Frequency-two refinement of every triangle, by the same midpoint construction used for F42, gives 20m+2 vertices and preserves the twelve degree-five vertices; every new midpoint has degree six. It makes all degree-five vertices independent. Thus there is an explicit infinite separated-curvature family, with unbounded axial distance, rather than only two isolated fixtures.

A possible adversarial mechanism is a chain of interior Kempe switches in the hexagonal cylinder. Two disconnected components of the *same colour pair* can be swapped independently and commute. If each switch leaves the root's coarse boundary connectivity unchanged, k switches could furnish many interior variants behind one boundary observation. A local lock at the cap would then interact with phase or connectivity along the cylinder. This would undermine charging warnings to root symmetries or finitely many boundary patterns. However, graph geometry alone does not establish that such proper four-colourings, locks, or warnings exist; this is a proposed mechanism, not a counterexample.

The sharp mathematical question is whether extension across a capped cylinder admits a bounded-state transfer invariant independent of its length. A positive answer might yield an interface invariant and a corridor compression lemma. A negative answer could supply an independent-switch obstruction to constant warning charges. Unlike adding more observation bits after a failed table, the cylinder gives a controlled parameter and a reason to expect growth.

The first analytical attack should derive the allowed colour transitions of one width-five annular layer and identify whether disjoint same-pair components can encode independent switches without changing the cap signature. This is smaller and more interpretable than enumerating all colourings of F32/F42. Any polynomial or bounded-warning conclusion still requires a preserved global invariant; neither Euler curvature nor symmetry supplies one by itself.
