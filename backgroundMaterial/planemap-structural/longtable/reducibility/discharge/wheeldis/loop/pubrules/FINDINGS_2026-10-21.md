## Finding 1: the 7 → 8 gap

In r58c and r58g the big link neighbour u pays is an **8**.
- Both published sets pay it with the same keys they use for a 7: RSST drule8, drule9, drule9m and drule20; 2026
  rule008, rule009 (both sides) and rule014. The target of each of these rules is "7+".
- Our l3q (RSST rule 9 restated, RELAY_SPEC_s21) is V = 7 only, so the main line has **no column at all** on these two rows.
- 7 → 8 was priced around 19-20 Oct against an older cert and was expected to die. Today's open rows are exactly the
  7-next-to-an-8 rows, so it is worth pricing again against the A78 cert.
- Candidate shape (own rule): l3q with V ∈ {7, 8, 9}, keys unchanged.

## Finding 2: longer and two-ended keys for 7 → 7

On the 7 → 7 face rows (r58b and the A74 rows), the first two units per side are inside l3q's key space: drule8 and
drule9 ≈ q(c ≤ 6, a = 5) and q(c ≤ 6, a ≤ 6, a2 = 5). The extra units come from two shapes l3q cannot express:
- **Longer one-sided key.** 2026 rule014 reads u's link from v as 6, 6, 5, 5 (out to link distance 4), with the apex
  over the first 6-6 pair ≥ 6. With it, the 2026 set leaves 7665566/6675676 and 7665566/6775676 at −2 (0.2 spare),
  where RSST sits at exactly 0. Rule020 (6, 6, 6, 5, 5 plus that apex) and rule019 (6, 5, 6, 5, 6 alternating) reach
  further still.
- **Two-sided and two-ended keys.**
  - RSST drule21 (target exactly 7) reads **both** sides of u's link at once: 6-, 6 on one side and 6+, *, 5, 5 round the
    other. It also needs the apex between the 6 and the target to be a 5.
  - 2026 rule021 and rule036 (target exactly 7) read into the **payee's own link**: after the shared 6, an apex 6,
    then a payee private 5, then a 6+.
  - Either way, the payee sees why it is being paid. l3q cannot express that with a one-sided key and a mask on u's
    private.

## General rule shape suggested

A big vertex (7, or 8 as in 2026 rule040-043) pays a big link neighbour of **any** degree ≥ 7.
- It pays once per side, keyed on its own link from that neighbour outward until a 5-5 pair (up to link distance 4).
- The key includes the apex over the first pair.
- Optionally, the key also reads the payee's own next two neighbours, to make the payee's credit exact.

The cheapest first test is V = 8 on r58c and r58g. Whether these shapes price negative is for our LP (Opus / Long
Table); this probe only shows that both published proofs pay exactly there.
