# Team A independent review of the proposed triangle-sum family

5 October 2026. Verdict: the hand argument is sound with the interface-component lemma stated explicitly. No larger graph was generated or enumerated.

For a clique-sum along a triangle F, restrict any whole bichromatic component of the union to one summand A. The intersection is empty or exactly one whole bichromatic component in A. Whole-component saturation follows directly from an A edge also being a global edge. Connectivity follows because every excursion through the other summand enters and exits through active vertices of F; they are either the same vertex or adjacent in the proper bichromatic clique. Replace such an excursion by that edge. Properness implies at most two active triangle vertices, but clique adjacency itself supplies the required shortcut.

The general short-fill theorem makes the lower-bound proof cleaner: any global mixed fill in at most two moves first converts to pure Kempe moves of no greater length at the original root. A degree-five root lies outside F, because an interface degree is d_A+d_B-2>=8. Its complete neighbourhood and every legal fan are unchanged. Extend each admitted hard A start to a full colouring of B by permuting B's palette to match the three distinct face colours. Project the resulting pure global moves to A, dropping empty intersections. The interface lemma gives valid whole A swaps, and a global target at this noninterface root is exactly an A target. This contradicts A's independently checked all-pair lower bound. There is no need to track moving holes at interface vertices.

For the upper bound, choose the last copy's root 0 and fan 1, outside all interfaces. Any admitted global start restricts to a proper admitted seed start. Each whole seed component lifts to the unique global component containing it; the same interface lemma proves its restriction is exactly that seed component, so no extra seed component is inadvertently swapped. Repeating the at-most-three seed swaps produces a target at the unchanged root. Canonical colour paths must be lifted to consistent actual colour labels before lifting the components; global quotient representatives do not themselves provide fixed palette labels.

The 34-start pure upper-bound histogram is a separate root-verified seed certificate; this note does not claim a new replay of it.

Consequently, conditional on the checked seed all-pair lower certificate and the checked root-0/fan-1 pure upper certificate, the chain of k copies has exactly m=3 and order 17k-3(k-1)=14k+3. Disjoint left/right faces ensure there is no unintended identification within a copy; gluing spherical triangulations along actual faces preserves a simple spherical triangulation.
