# Wave 2 — motivation

**2 October 2026.** The September wave was stopped for cost. The computations it finished are still on disk. This note is for the managers who are turning those files into reports.

## Affirmation

M-Kempe can already separate a killed universal sentence from a weaker one that the same machine still supports. K4's skill is the length gap $s_1 - d$. K5's skill is telling a connected reconfiguration graph from a monotone path. K6's skill is telling Kempe's two-swap procedure from the full Kempe class: the Errera graph can kill the first and leave the second standing.

M-Algebra's skill is a polynomial you can evaluate. A root in $(3,4)$ is a fact about the real line. It is not a zero at $4$.

M-Duality's skill is an identity checked four ways. A reformulation is a labelled door. The room behind it is still the Four Colour Theorem, and saying so is the work.

## Time-out

Use this when a draft starts calling a finite count a theorem, or a reformulation a proof.

Take the smallest graph in the file, $K_4$ or $T_{5,0}$. Colour it by hand on one card. On the back write the one number the program returned. If the sentence on the front is about every planar graph and the number on the back is about one graph, send the draft back.

The card does not replace the kill criterion.

## The lighthouse and the tide table

A keeper on a black rock kept two books. One was a tide table printed for every harbour he had never seen. The other was a pencil log of the water at his own step, hour by hour, for eleven years. A visitor read the printed table, saw that the water had never touched the fourth mark in those eleven years, and wrote to the board that the sea could not reach it.

The keeper took him down at dusk. The step was dry. The log agreed. Then she showed him a single line in the printed table, from a harbour three days' sail north, where the water had stood at $3.6$ and the next column was blank. "That mark is not my step," she said. "And my step is not the sea."

He wanted the eleven years to finish the argument. She sent him back up with the pencil log under his arm and the printed table left on the bench. The log was allowed to say what it had measured. It was not allowed to speak for a coast it had not visited.

**Lesson.** A report is finished when the measured range, the killed sentence, and the sentence still open are three different lines. Eleven years at one rock is evidence. It is not the ocean.
