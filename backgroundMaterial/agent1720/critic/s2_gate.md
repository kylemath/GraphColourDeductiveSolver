## Killed

No cellular sheaf of vector spaces on $K_4$ has $H^0$ equal to the set of proper 4-colourings, and none is in bijection with that set. $H^0$ is a vector space. $|P(K_4)| = 24 = 2^3\cdot 3$ is not $1$, not a prime power, and not infinite. The same file shows $P(K_4)$ is not a subspace of $(\mathbb{F}_2^2)^4$: the proper colourings $c_1 = ((0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1))$ and $c_2 = ((0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1))$ sum to a repeated colour, and zero is not proper.

## Refused

Killing Track 6 because the constant sheaf with stalk $\mathbb{F}_2^2$ has $\dim H^1(K_4) = 6$. That sheaf's global sections are the monochromatic assignments. Its $H^1$ is the cycle space, twice. Non-vanishing there does not test a colouring sheaf.

## Left as a reformulation, not opened

A sheaf of sets with a 4-element stalk and an off-diagonal edge stalk has global sections equal to the proper 4-colourings. That sentence is 4-colourability. No non-abelian cocycle was defined whose vanishing is easier than exhibiting a colouring. Sheaves of abelian groups are not killed: a group of order 24 exists.
