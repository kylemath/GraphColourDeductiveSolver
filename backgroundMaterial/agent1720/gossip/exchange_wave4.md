# Exchange wave 4

Written after the wave-4 reports were on disk. `exchange_wave3.md` said A2, D2, D3, S3, and S4 had no slips, and that the wave-4 slips were missing. Those reports exist. This note records only the facts that were not in the wave-3 exchange.

- A2: outerplanar and series-parallel graphs have \(P(G,4)>0\); every planar graph has \(P(G,k)>0\) for integers \(k \ge 6\); every 3-tree has \(P(G,4)=24\). \(P(G,4)>0\) for every planar graph is the Four Colour Theorem. `groups/A2_report.md`
- D2 and D2b: the signed Penrose sum equals \((-1)^n\) times the Tait count on all 1555 triangulations. The 1305 negative cases are the odd-order graphs. `groups/D2_report.md`, `groups/D2b_report.md`
- D3: every Tait colouring of the dual of a triangulation on \(n \ge 4\) vertices has sign \((-1)^n\). The census through \(n \le 8\) agrees. Non-vanishing is still existence. `groups/D3_report.md`, `critic/d3_sign_gate.md`
- S3 and S3b: the Birkhoff diamond is D-reducible (729 ring colourings, empty maximal bad set). A single degree-5 vertex is not (30 bad colourings remain). Free Kempe-component swaps accept both graphs and are not this test. `groups/S3_report.md`, `groups/S3b_report.md`
- S4: the icosahedron kills “every triangulation has a vertex of degree at most 4”. Hajós at \(k=5\), odd Hadwiger at \(t=5\), and a Petersen minor in every snark stay open. `groups/S4_report.md`
- A3 and S4b: a simple plane triangulation is 3-colourable if and only if every degree is even. S4b is a second proof of the same statement. `groups/A3_report.md`, `groups/S4b_report.md`
- L-chain: if \(c(v) \notin \{a,b\}\), \((a,b)\)-reachability in \(G\) equals reachability in \(G-v\). `groups/Lchain_report.md`
- L-deg4: a separated neighbour pair lets one Kempe swap free a colour at a degree-4 vertex. Existence of that pair is still a hypothesis. `groups/Ldeg4_report.md`
- F5: the Five Colour Theorem is proved in prose. Euler’s formula and Jordan’s theorem are cited from Diestel. There is no Lean proof. `groups/F5_report.md`
- K7: Fritsch is \(T_{9,49}\). KC5 holds at its six degree-5 vertices, and the fixing distance is 2. Tilley’s order-12 triangulation has no published edge list. `groups/K7_report.md`
- Gauge: arXiv:2412.18558 is blocked on the Wolfram dumps. Mathematica is not installed. `groups/G2_report.md`
