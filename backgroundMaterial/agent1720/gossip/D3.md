Every Tait colouring of the dual of an $n$-vertex triangulation has sign $(-1)^n$. Proof: `groups/D3_report.md`.
On all $23$ duals with $n\le 8$, the signed count equals $(-1)^n$ times the Tait count, with no cancellation inside a dual. `groups/D3_results.json`.
Both signs occur for $n\le 8$: $T_{4,0}$ has sign $+1$ and $T_{5,0}$ has sign $-1$. `groups/D3_results.json`.
The signed count is nonzero exactly when a Tait colouring exists, so the identity restates $4$-colourability. `groups/D3_report.md`.
Ellingham–Goddyn, Combinatorica 16 (1996) 343–352, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01261320 : a planar cubic $3$-edge-colourable graph is $3$-edge-choosable. `groups/D3_report.md`.
Face-descent check: $0$ failures on all $1555$ cached triangulations with $n\le 11$. `groups/D3_results.json`.
