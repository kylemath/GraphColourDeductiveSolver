## The target

The quarter floor: in every Kempe class of T − v at a degree-5 hole, at least 1/4 of the states are filled. In its strong per-j form this is U_j ≤ F_{j+1} + F_{j+3} + F_{j+4}.
- Lemma A (the non-DL half) is audited.
- What remains open is the DL half, |DD_j| ≤ room_j. It holds in every class tested and is genuinely global: no fixed-radius local charging proves it.
- The aim tonight is a global argument (Euler counting, charges, topology) or a decisive negative.

## Results

(appended as jobs finish)

### W1-A Euler pressure: negative (c1703b2, local-runs/15-euler-pressure/)
- 243,727 states: all degree-5 holes at orders 12–20, plus the order-22 F-cycle hole.
- DL states are NOT closer to Euler saturation in any bipartite subgraph tried: B_μ, B_α, the lock union U, μ-vs-{A,B}.
  - No DL state has slack 0 in U or in μ-vs-{A,B}; the minimum is 4.
  - 4,883 non-DL states do reach slack 0 in U.
- Modest effects for DL versus non-DL:
  - Kawarabayashi–Yoneda penalty sum lower by 3.2 (d = −1.09);
  - KY total lower by 1.75 (d = −0.90);
  - slack at μ lower by 1.5 (d = −0.70).
- Reading: a single-state Euler count cannot see the locks, so any Euler argument would have to be class-level, a sum over states. The question is passed to W1-B.

### W1-C Charges versus the floor: negative (3d8f08d, local-runs/17-charges-floor/)
- 2,469 classes, 391,173 states; every degree-5 hole at orders 12–20 plus all 419 floor classes.
- The capped charge q(v) = ±3 is forced by the link pattern alone (0 failures), so its sign is only a gauge, not a class invariant.
- Gauge-free: W = ε·(signed face count) satisfies W = 4τ − 3 on filled states (τ = σ·deg) and W ≡ 3 mod 4 on unfilled states (observed).
- In the 405 four-block floor classes the block sums of link charges obey exact relations (e.g. the lock-end charge flips sign relative to the filled block), but their values vary class by class: no class-constant charge invariant separates the blocks.
- No exact identity Σ(charge statistic) = αF + βU + γD exists. The near-identity Σ_unfilled W = Σ_filled W holds in 412 of 419 floor classes and also in 16 non-floor classes, so it neither characterises nor implies the floor.
- Reading: Heawood charges map where pressure moves but carry no conserved quantity that bounds the filled fraction. A charge proof of the floor is closed for now.

### W2-D Correlation laws by degree (63b7e77, df8adb2; local-runs/19-correlation-laws/)
- Orders 12–24, all holes of degree 5, 6, 7; own C++ (matches the old degree-5 counts on all 13,712 holes).
- Degree 5: exactly ONE valid single-term family, U_j ≤ F_{j+1} + F_{j+3} + F_{j+4}; its LP gives exactly 1/4.
- Degree 6: 5 unfilled orbits; smallest valid S has 5 of 11 filled words; LP floor 1/12 versus observed 1/8.
- Degree 7: 7 unfilled orbits; smallest S of size 5–6 of 21; LP floor 4/85 versus observed 2/17.
- The valid families keep shrinking as orders are added (not saturated), and the LP floors fall. So single-term laws explain the floor only at degree 5; degrees 6 and 7 would need joint or weighted laws.
- Cross-degree: the smallest S always contains every filled word one swap away from the unfilled type, plus extras: 1 extra at degree 5 (F_{j+1}, two swaps away), 2–4 at degree 6, 3–5 at degree 7.
- Reading: degree 5 is special because the law is rigid (exactly one family, exactly one extra term). That extra term F_{j+1} is the DL half. The structure does not generalise cleanly, which is consistent with the floor being degree-5-specific.
