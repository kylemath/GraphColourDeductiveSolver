$$
I=\{x:x\text{ is coloured in the current deletion},\ x\neq 17,\ \text{and }c(x)=c^0(x)\},
$$

and $|I|$ takes the values $23,22,16,12$. Lemma 1.3(b) in the vacancy note says every move is reversible by a move of the same kind, so $M(T)$ is undirected. A function of the current state alone cannot strictly fall on every edge. The forward walk of $|I|$ is a rise on the reverse walk, $12,16,22,23$, against the same remembered $c^0$. Survival of the forward walk is a lead about that walk. It is not a Lyapunov function of the state.

**Ranker.** The same four values are the path test, and the agreement cardinality passes it. The sentence names no rail, no pole, and no unwrapped index, and it is defined at the degree-$6$ hole. The sentence the Ranker will not water down is that $23,22,16,12$ is a score of the published walk.

**Physicist.** Two integers were put through the kill test and both die. The defect count, the number of singleton colours on the link, is $3,2,2,1$: it falls on the slide, stays $2$ across the swap that cuts $P$, and falls only at the fill. ## Where the positions disagree

The Ranker treats the agreement cardinality as the lead the kill rule asked for. The four values are defined, and each of the three steps is a strict drop. On the Ranker's reading, that is survival of the saved path, and the navigator's attack page priced survival of that path at a raise of the research plan to Medium, still short of a theorem.

The Witness refuses that reading of the word potential. The belt potential falls on the returns a rule names. $|I|$ falls on a walk a search already printed. The reverse walk is a legal walk, by the same lemma the belt argument uses, and $|I|$ rises on it. A ranking of one directed traversal of an undirected graph is a score. The Witness will not let the joint page call $|I|$ a Lyapunov function of $M(T)$.

The Physicist refuses a third thing. The defect count and the chain length were the sentences that still talked about the move: a singleton colour is a legal slide, and the chain is what the swap cuts. Both are constant on a step that stands between the start and the fill. $|I|$ has no such reading. It remembers $c^0$ and counts disagreements after the fact. The Physicist will not let a dead defect count back in through a lexicographic pair whose second coordinate is $|I|$. On the step where the defect count stays $2$, any drop of the pair is the drop of $|I|$.

The Ranker answers that this is already the position on the pair: the excess waits until the fill, and the lead is one lead. The Ranker does not claim a second survivor. The Ranker does claim that the navigator's sentence — surviving the path raises the plan to Medium, and is not a theorem — applies to this integer, because the kill page defined survival as four values and three strict drops.

The Witness answers that the kill page also says matching that standard is not a theorem, and that next step 4 is still open: the five-swap Kempe path at the same hole. A quantity that descends on that longer route has not selected the short path. The Physicist answers that a raise to Medium is a raise for a potential, and the belt page's potential is the integer together with the next-zero rule. The integer alone is the screen. The screen is what the positions already called a lead.

They agree on the arithmetic that is already in the positions: $|I|$ is $23,22,16,12$ on the mixed path; the defect count is $3,2,2,1$; the $\beta\gamma$-chain is $8$ then $8$; the graph does not change. They do not agree on what the first of those sequences is worth.
