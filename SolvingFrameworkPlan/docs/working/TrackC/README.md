## 2. Scope lemma (§6.4): `PlaneMap/FrameScope.lean` [formal]

Hypotheses: `T` triangulated with `NoSep T`, `h` a vertex with `deg h = 5`, and `P : Pent T.graph h`. Minimum degree 5 is **not** needed.

- `tips_not_adj`, `link_tips_not_adj`: `¬ T.Adj (P.x j) (P.x (j+2))`.
  - Proof: the three triangles at `h` would all be facial, so the rotation at `h` would contain a 3-cycle (`chain5`).
  - The paper's justification ("separating triangle, given minimum degree 5") is right in spirit. The formal proof uses `NoSep` and `deg h = 5` only.
- `appears_diamond_of_run`: if `x j`, `x (j+1)`, `x (j+2)` all have degree 5, then `Appears ![5,5,5,5] T ![h, x j, x (j+1), x (j+2)]`. The centres are `h` and `x (j+1)`.
- `appears_c2122_of_run`: if the degrees are `(5,6,5)`, then `Appears ![6,5,5,5] T ![x (j+1), x j, h, x (j+2)]`. The degree-6 vertex `x (j+1)` is centre 0, matching `C2122Appears`.
- `LinkTipsClean P j`: every common neighbour of `x j` and `x (j+2)` is `h` or `x (j+1)`. This is exactly `TipsClean` for both labellings.
- `occ_diamond_of_run` and `occ_c2122_of_run`: an `Occ` in one of the two orientations, via the existing `occ_of_appears`.
- `no_555_run`, from `DiamondFree`, and `no_565_run`, from `Conf2122Free`: under `LinkTipsClean P j` the corresponding run does not occur.

Axioms: `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

**Verdict on the §6.4 claims.** Both the 555 claim and the (5,6,5) claim are true as hand statements about appearances. Formally, they exclude the run from $\mathcal F$ only under `LinkTipsClean`. I found nothing false. One wording note: "frame-class holes have no cyclic 555 and no cyclic 565" holds formally only with clean tips. Without F2 it is not a theorem about $\mathcal F$ as defined. The paper's "Formal caveat" bullet already says this; the next bullet ("So frame-class holes have no …") should carry the same qualifier.

## 3. Non-vacuity witness: `PlaneMap/FrameWit22Map.lean` + `PlaneMap/FrameWit22.lean` [formal]

- **The map.** It is `gentri/tri22.txt` line 642 (0-based 641). It is the smallest of the six configuration-free graphs found at orders ≤ 24, re-found by `find.py` (scratch).
  - 22 vertices: 12 of degree 5 and 10 of degree 6.
  - Its degree-5 vertices are not independent, so it is not an IPR dual.
- **How it was built.**
  - `FrameWit22Map.lean` was generated by the existing `StudioMathReview-scripts/gen_smap.py`. The face input is in `TrackC/FrameWit22-faces.json`.
  - `Fills` is proved by the linear certificate.
  - It took about 63 s to compile.
- **Theorems.** `connected`, `noSep`, `min_degree`, `diamondFree`, `conf2122Free`, `frameClass` (the full hypothesis list of `RStarFrame`) and **`frameClass_nonempty`**: `∃ m (T : SphericalMap m), 0 < m ∧ connected ∧ triangulated ∧ min degree 5 ∧ NoSep ∧ DiamondFree ∧ Conf2122Free`.
- **How freeness is proved.** Every `Occ` (any of the four) gives an edge `int 0 ~ int 2` with distinct common neighbours `int 1` and `int 3`, carrying the configuration's degrees. A kernel `decide` over the tables (`no_shape`) shows that no such quadruple exists.
- **Axioms:** `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
- **What this does not show.** The witness says nothing about R\*: it does not show that the map has a pure-clean degree-5 vertex. That would be a separate (`RadiusFive`-style) certificate.

## 4. F2 (separating 4-cycle): assessment and plan. Not started.

**What F2 would buy.** A lemma `colorable_of_separating_4cycle`: if `T` is a triangulation with a separating 4-cycle `abcd`, and every map of smaller support is 4-colourable, then `T` is 4-colourable. With it:

- `four_color_of_RStarFrame` could add "no separating 4-cycle" to the class.
- `TipsClean` would become automatic, so `Occ`-free would equal appearance-free.
- `no_555_run` and `no_565_run` would hold in $\mathcal F$ outright.

**Pieces the library already has.**

- The `TriPotential` / `keep` side machinery (`SideTriangle.lean`, `MinimalFrame.lean`) for a 3-cycle.
- `exists_fills_chord` and `exists_spherical_chord` (`SphericalChordInsert`).
- `RingFace`, `chains_noncrossing` and `chains_noncrossing_same` (`RingChains.lean`), stated for a ring face of any length `r`, so they cover `r = 4`.
- `face_alternating_walks_meet` (`RingJordan.lean`).
- The `ConfigOcc`-style "delete the interior, the ring becomes a face" construction (`*Occ.lean`).

**Work packages.**

1. **Sides of a 4-cycle.** Generalise `triInd` and `TriPotential` to an even cycle indicator, which is the same `Fills` argument. Define `keep` for the 4-cycle and prove the covering lemma: every edge lies on one side. Medium; mostly a copy of D2/D3 with four vertices.
2. **Each side is a smaller spherical map with the 4-cycle as a face.** Reuse the deletion route of `configOcc`: delete the other side's interior vertices, then show `abcd` is a face, as in the k-cycle version of G0. Medium to hard. The existing `ConfigOcc` proofs are generated per configuration, so a generic k-ring version may need to be written.
3. **Insert a chosen chord** (`ac` or `bd`) into that 4-face to get a triangulation of smaller support, and colour it by the induction hypothesis. Medium.
4. **Birkhoff's ring-matching case analysis on the 4-face.**
   - The ring patterns are `a = c`, `b = d`, or all four distinct.
   - A mismatch is repaired by one Kempe swap on one side.
   - The swap can only fail if the alternate pair is joined, which `chains_noncrossing` with `r = 4` forbids.
   - Then permute the colours and glue, as in `colorable_of_separating_triangle`.
   - Hard. This is the main new proof, a finite case split over at most about 6 pattern pairs.
5. **Wire it into the frame.** Add a `NoSep4` hypothesis to a new `RStarFrame4` and prove `rStarFrame4 → 4CT` and `RStarFrame → RStarFrame4`. Then derive `TipsClean` from `NoSep4` for the two appearance shapes. That last step is a short rotation argument: a common neighbour `y` of the tips other than the centres gives the 4-cycle `tip–h–tip–y`, separating `x (j+1)` from the rest. Easy once 1–4 exist.

**Estimate and risks.** Several focused days, consistent with PLAN.md's "hard (days)". Package 2 (a generic k-ring deletion) is the riskiest and should be prototyped first.

One open hypothesis question needs Math's answer first: whether the 4-cycle `abcd` may have a chord in `T`, i.e. `ac ∈ T`. If it has one, the triangles `abc` and `acd` are separating unless they are faces, so `NoSep` excludes most of these cases. This should be settled before step 1.
