## Part A. The model and the headline statements

### A1. What the model assumes

1. **Graphs and colourings.** These are Mathlib's `SimpleGraph (Fin n)` and `G.Colorable 4`.
2. **Spherical maps.** A spherical map is the library's `SphericalMap n` (PlaneMap snapshot). It
   has three parts:
   - `graph`, a simple graph;
   - `rotation : RotationSystem graph`, a permutation `next` of the darts that fixes each
     dart's tail and is cyclic on the darts at each vertex;
   - `fills : rotation.Fills`.

   `Fills` says that every mod-2 edge combination that is even at every vertex is a face sum:
   `φ (edge d) = c (faceOf d) + c (faceOf d.symm)` for some `c : Face → ZMod 2`.

   **`Fills` is the only planarity assumption.** The model is combinatorial maps. Connectedness
   is not part of the carrier.

   The library proves (JordanEven) that maps generated by its plane-map constructions satisfy
   `Fills`. The step from a topological plane embedding to a `SphericalMap` is not part of
   this development.
3. **Faces and triangulations.**
   - `faceNext d = next d.symm`.
   - `Triangulated M` means every face has length 3.
   - `Facial T p q r := Nx T q p r ∨ Nx T p q r`, where `Nx T u v w` says that `w` follows `v`
     in the rotation at `u`.
   - `NoSep T` says that every triangle is facial.
4. **Jordan facts are proved, not assumed.**
   - `rotation_arc_separation` (at a vertex) and `face_alternating_walks_meet` (across a simple
     face) are derived from `Fills` by mod-2 loop potentials.
   - `chains_noncrossing` and `chains_noncrossing_same` (Kempe chains on a ring face) follow
     from them.
5. **Kempe semantics.** These are library definitions, `VacancyShortFill`:
   - `ProperOff G h c`: `c` is proper on `G − h`.
   - `pairGraph G h c a b`: the `{a,b}`-subgraph of `G − h`.
   - `Whole`: one *entire* component of `pairGraph`.
   - `KempeStep`: swap `a ↔ b` on one whole component, for any pair `a ≠ b`.
   - `PurePath`, `PureFill G h c m`: some fill is reached within `m` steps.
   - `Target`: a colour is missing on the link of `h`.

   The project's predicate is `PureClean T r := ∀ c, ProperOff T.graph r c → ∃ m, PureFill T.graph r c m`.
6. **Configuration occurrence.** An occurrence is the structure `Occ T ring int`, generated per
   configuration and orientation (`DiamondM/P`, `C2122M/P`). It requires:
   - an injective ring;
   - an injective interior, disjoint from the ring;
   - the free-completion degrees at the interior vertices;
   - the full rotation at each interior vertex, as `Nx` facts.

   Ring chords are allowed. **Not formalised:** the step from the RSST notion "appears"
   (induced subgraph, faces, degrees) to an `Occ` with distinct ring vertices. That step
   uses internal 6-connectivity.

### A2. Conditional theorems: R\* implies the Four Colour Theorem

Each line gives the hypothesis and then the theorem, with conclusion `∀ n (M : SphericalMap n), M.graph.Colorable 4`.

| Theorem | Hypothesis, exactly | Module |
|---|---|---|
| `four_color_of_RStarFrame` | `RStarFrame`: every `T : SphericalMap m` with `0 < m`, `T.graph.Connected`, `T.Triangulated`, `∀ x, 5 ≤ T.graph.degree x`, `NoSep T`, `DiamondFree T`, `Conf2122Free T` has `v` with `T.graph.degree v = 5 ∧ PureClean T v` | `FrameF3` |
| `four_color_of_RStar_noSepTri` | `RStarNoSepTri`: the same without `DiamondFree` and `Conf2122Free` | `MinimalFrame` |
| `four_color_of_core_Rstar` | `RStarCore`: every connected triangulation with a facial triangle `p q r`, `NoSep T`, and `5 ≤ degree x` off `{p,q,r}` has `CleanOff T p q r` (a pure-clean degree-5 vertex off the face) | `RStarCore` |
| `four_color_of_RStarSupport` | `RStarSupport` (the relative class stated on the support) | `SideTriangle` |
| `four_color_of_global_Rstar` | R\* for all connected triangulations of minimum degree 5 | `RStar` |

The implications between the hypotheses are compiled:
- `rStarNoSepTri_of_core : RStarCore → RStarNoSepTri`;
- `rStarFrame_of_noSepTri : RStarNoSepTri → RStarFrame`;
- `rStarSupport_of_core : RStarCore → RStarSupport`.

So `RStarFrame` is the **weakest** of these hypotheses.

`DiamondFree T` means there is no `DiamondM.Occ T ring int` and no `DiamondP.Occ T ring int`.
`Conf2122Free T` is the same for 2.122.

**The frame proof** (`four_color_of_RStarFrame`) is by strong induction on the support size,
through `four_color_of_smaller_gate`, with four cases:
- (F1) a separating triangle is reduced by gluing, `colorable_of_separating_triangle`;
- (F3) a diamond or 2.122 occurrence is reduced by its D-reducibility certificate,
  `X.colorable_of_occ`;
- (F4) otherwise, the pure-clean vertex is deleted, the rest coloured, and the vertex extended,
  `extend_of_pureClean`.

### A3. Unconditional theorems (headline)

**Vacancy (Kempe) theorems:**
- `theorem_H`: a triangulated map; `h` of degree 5 with all link vertices of degree 5; no separating triangle at `h`; `ProperOff M.graph h c` ⟹ `PureFill M.graph h c 3`.
- `theorem_HP`: the same, but one link vertex `p` has arbitrary degree ⟹ `PureFill M.graph h c 6`.
- `pureClean_of_theorem_H`, `pureClean_of_theorem_HP`: the same hypotheses give `PureClean T h`.
- `extend_of_pureClean`: `PureClean T r → (T.graph.induce {z | z ≠ r}).Colorable 4 → T.graph.Colorable 4`.

**Reductions:**
- `colorable_of_separating_triangle`: a triangulated `T` with a non-facial triangle `p q r`, plus four-colourability of every map with smaller support, gives `T.graph.Colorable 4`.
- `DiamondM/DiamondP/C2122M/C2122P.colorable_of_occ`: a triangulated `T` with `Occ T ring int`, plus the same smaller-support hypothesis, gives `T.graph.Colorable 4`. These are the D-reducibility of the Birkhoff diamond and of RSST 2.122, in both orientations.

**Counting:**
- `edge_card_bound_sharp`: `|E| + 2 ≤ n + |F|`.
- `twelve_light_fives`: a triangulation of minimum degree 5 has `≥ 12` light fives.
- `relative_light_fives`: the relative bound with at most three protected vertices of degree 4.

**Jordan:** `rotation_arc_separation`, `face_alternating_walks_meet`, `chains_noncrossing`,
`chains_noncrossing_same` (see A1.4).

### A4. Sanity checks [sanity]

**Icosahedron** (`RStarSanity`, namespace `SimpleGraph.Icosahedron`), on the library's
`Icosahedron.sphericalMap`:
- `mem_class`: it is in the class of `RStarNoSepTri`. It has `0 < 12` vertices, is connected and triangulated, has minimum degree 5, and satisfies `NoSep`.
- `pureClean v`: **every vertex is pure-clean** (by Theorem H). `rStar_conclusion` is R\*'s conclusion.
- `occ_DiamondM`, `occ_DiamondP`: both diamond orientations occur, with explicit labellings, so `Occ` is not vacuous. Hence `not_diamondFree`: the icosahedron is outside the class of `RStarFrame` and is handled by F3.
- `conf2122Free`: 2.122 does not occur. It needs an interior vertex of degree 6.

These are checks of the **definitions**: the class is non-empty, `PureClean` is satisfiable,
and `Occ` is satisfiable. They are **not evidence for R\***. Per the audit (15:51, §4), a hole
whose deletion has a single Kempe class is pure-clean automatically.

**Radius 5** (`RadiusFive`). The audited certificates are `80b930d1540e4ee3` (order 32, hole 23) and `91a307d1852a1764` (order 28, hole 22). Each graph is entered from the certificate's face list. For each certificate state `c0`:
- `proper`: `c0` is proper off the hole.
- `unfilled`: the hole's link carries all four colours.
- `pureFill`: `PureFill G hole c0 5`, by an explicit five-step path of whole-component swaps.

An independent breadth-first search under the same semantics (`radius_bfs.py`) finds **no fill
within 4** from either state. The formal radius is therefore exactly 5, matching the certificate.
That lower bound is computed, not compiled. These checks are at graph level: that the graphs
are spherical triangulations is certified by the producer's checker.

### A5. Not formalised (explicit gaps)

- **R\*** itself. Every route to the Four Colour Theorem here is conditional on a form of R\* (A2).
- **The step from "appears" to `Occ`** (A1.6). This is the audit's bridge lemma (15:51, §2).
  Of its parts:
  - "G is the deletion, as a `SphericalMap`" and "the ring is a face of G" are compiled, for
    `Occ`, as `X.configOcc`;
  - the step from the RSST "appears" to an `Occ` with distinct ring vertices is open.

  `four_color_of_RStarFrame` does not need the bridge, because its hypothesis is stated with
  `Occ`-freeness. Without the bridge, `RStarFrame`'s class is larger than "no RSST
  appearance". It still excludes every `Occ`.
- **F2, the separating 4-cycle.** It needs a contraction or star construction and is waiting on
  Math. It is not used by any theorem above. The R\* classes keep separating 4-cycles.
- **The step from a topological embedding to `SphericalMap`** (A1.2).
