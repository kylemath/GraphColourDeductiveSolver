What each step says

Open gap
Chain count alternates (NRC)

Track J pinned the shape down. Such a class is made of 20-state cycles alternating 8 and 9 chains, with the extra chain always the hole's α–μ swap. Excluding it reduces to NRC: on the sphere, no π-cycle consists of doubly-locked states with at most 9 chains. Across 473k sphere holes the longest such run is 7 (a cycle needs 10). It is false in general graphs even with triangles everywhere and the H3 balance, so it needs planarity itself, like rigid isolation.

Open gap
LPC on the sphere

No Kempe class consists only of closed doubly-locked π-cycles. This would make every hole pure-clean, which is more than "no frozen orbits" needs. No counterexample anywhere, including 2,772 parity-clean cycle classes on five other surfaces.

Proved in Lean
Chain-parity law

On the sphere, every π-step between doubly-locked states changes the number of Kempe chains by an odd amount. Formal since 8 Oct (chainParityLaw_sphere, through Conjecture F and Tutte's identity, no topology needed). It also has an independently reviewed hand proof by curve surgery. It fails on the torus and projective plane, as it must.

Proved in Lean
Conjecture F

F follows from Tutte's parity theorem and Fisk's degree, with the hole filled by the two lock diagonals. Lean already proves that F implies the chain-parity law (chainParityLaw_of_F), F is where that route uses planarity. Hand proof reviewed correct; now formal (conjectureF, TutteSides.lean, ChainF.lean), using Tutte's identity proved by linear algebra mod 2.

Proved in Lean
Lemma W, corrected

How the clockwise-face count changes under a Kempe swap. The first version was false for some swaps; the corrected form holds on any oriented surface and covers the two moves the proof needs. ChainMod4.lean

Proved in Lean
Lock parity

A lock holds exactly when a two-colour Kempe component has an odd number of odd-degree vertices. This is where planarity enters. QuarterLockParity.lean

Proved in Lean
No frozen orbits ⇒ R* ⇒ 4CT

rStarFrame_of_no_allDL_orbit and four_color_of_RStarFrame. The statements are frozen in Mathlib-only challenge files with anti-drift bridges.

Review pending
Violators = path ends

In a class with no filled state, the states that break lock parity are exactly the loose ends of π-paths. So on the sphere a bad class would have to be made of closed cycles.

Deferred
Drawing ⇒ combinatorial map

Our theorem is about combinatorial maps. Drawn planar graphs need a topology bridge, and OpenAI's Barnette formalisation plus schoenflies-lean cover most of it. It waits until the red boxes move.

Routes ruled out

  • Small unavoidable set. The best proved set has 59 hole types, and the all-6 type is forced with nothing local to handle it.
  • All-6 holes via many holes or single orbits. Holes don't compensate each other.
  • Barnette-style signed sum. No phase flips on both lock swaps and Kempe cubes.
  • Stream function on states. The class-level potential is just the floor restated.
  • Hole-local proof of LPC. A 28-vertex non-planar graph satisfies every local fact with no filled state.
  • Per-cycle chain from 6–7 Oct. A₃₄′, W2, Lemma S, statement (c) and universal B′ are false at degree 6.
