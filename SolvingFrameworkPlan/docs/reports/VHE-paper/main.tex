\section*{How this paper was made: disclosure of AI authorship}
\textbf{This work was produced by a team of AI agents, not by a human mathematician working alone.} The mathematics (definitions, hand proofs, counterexamples), the Lean~4 formalisation, the computations and the text of this paper were produced by instances of Claude, an AI model family by Anthropic, run through Claude Code in several separate sessions with named roles: \emph{Long Table} (hand proofs and exploratory experiments), \emph{Math} (proof review, Lean formalisation, module audit, written go-aheads for experiments), \emph{Audit} (independent replays and adversarial review), \emph{Navigator} (the status ledger and its status words), a \emph{coordinator} session (sequencing between teams), and a \emph{night swarm} of overnight exploratory agents whose outputs are used only as leads. Several of these sessions delegated sub-tasks to further short-lived AI sub-agents, for example to write checkers blind to the producer's code. The model versions varied between sessions and were not recorded uniformly [\textsc{to complete if wanted}].

\textbf{How results were checked.} Each claim carries one label. \lab{compiled} means a Lean development with no \texttt{sorry} and only the standard axioms, rechecked by a module audit run by the Audit team. \lab{hand} means a written argument reviewed by another AI session, not by a human referee. \lab{computed} means a declared experiment whose producer was checked by a separately written checker, with the declaration hashed before the run, and which says only ``on these graphs''. \lab{cited} is taken from the literature and was read by AI agents, not by the author. \lab{open} is not claimed. The labels come from this process, and the Navigator's ledger gives each result's status word; no claim here is stronger than that ledger. Reviews among AI sessions can share blind spots, which is why the \lab{hand} results should be read as unrefereed.
