## 4. Plan (no code): the RSST "appears" → `Occ` bridge with distinct ring vertices

**Target, per configuration K ∈ {diamond, 2.122}:**
- Hypotheses:
  - T triangulated;
  - `NoSep T`;
  - `NoSep4ι` (below);
  - `Appears T K ι`.
- Conclusion: ∃ ε ring, `K_ε.Occ T ring ι`.

**Definition `Appears T K ι`** (RSST: induced, faces, degrees), stated without rotations:
- ι is injective;
- `T.Adj (ι u) (ι w) ↔ intAdj u w`;
- `degree (ι u) = γ u`;
- each interior triangle of K is `Facial` in T.

Both configurations have interior K₄ − e, so there are two interior triangles, sharing the centre edge.

**Steps** (sizes are estimates):

| Step | Content | How | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 orientation | One ε for all interior vertices: the two faces on the centre edge lie on opposite sides | generic lemma | ~40 lines |
| B2 rotation slots | At ι u the rotation has period `degree = γ u`, so slots 0…γ−1 are distinct. The library already has `RotationSystem.neighbor_period_eq_degree` (`RotationSystem.lean:635`) | generic | ~60 lines |
| B3 ring and `Nx` facts | Define `ring t` by iterating `next` at a chosen interior vertex (the fan table `gen_occ.py` already emits). The 20 or 21 `Nx` fields are then definitional or follow by `nx_tri` closure | extend `gen_occ.py` | ~150 lines per configuration and orientation |
| B4 disjointness | A ring slot holding ι w would repeat ι w in ι u's rotation (by inducedness it already has an interior slot). Contradiction with B2 | generated | ~40 lines each |
| B5 ring injectivity | 15 pairs (diamond) and 21 (2.122). Detail below | generated case split, plus one generic 4-cycle lemma (~100–200 lines) | ~300–500 lines each |

**B5 in detail.** Suppose ring s = ring t = x, with x in the slot of interior a (position s) and of interior b (position t). There are three cases:
- **a = b:** two slots are equal, against B2.
- **a ~ b:** the triangle x a b is facial by `NoSep`, so x is the third vertex of a face on ab. That vertex is interior (against B4) or a single ring position (against s ≠ t, by B2).
- **d(a, b) = 2:** the two tips, through a centre c, give a 4-cycle x a c b.
  - If one side of it is empty, it has a chord. The chord ab is excluded by inducedness. The chord xc repeats a slot of c, against B2.
  - If it separates, a hypothesis is needed.

The interior diameter is 2, so no longer cycles arise. **For these two configurations internal 6-connectivity is not needed. What is needed is no separating triangle (`NoSep`, available) and no separating 4-cycle through three interior vertices (`NoSep4ι`, not available).**

**Total:**
- About 200 lines generic, plus 4 × (500–700) lines generated: roughly **2,000–3,000 lines**, mostly generated by extending `gen_occ.py`.
- Compile cost similar to the `…Occ` files, seconds to a minute each.

**The real cost is the 4-cycle.** There are two ways to discharge `NoSep4ι`:
- **(a) Add it to the class and prove it.** Add "no separating 4-cycle" to `RStarFrame`'s class and compile Birkhoff's separating-4-cycle reduction (A5's F2, open, waiting on Math). This is the larger and riskier piece, perhaps 800–1,500 lines, possibly reusing `RingChains`/`RingReduce` at r = 4.
- **(b) State the bridge under `NoSep4ι` and do not prove it.** This gives "within NoSep ∧ NoSep4 triangulations, `Occ`-free ⇔ no RSST appearance of these two configurations". The class gap of A5 then becomes exactly the separating 4-cycles.

**Recommendation:** do (b) first (mechanical), and keep F2 as its own item.
