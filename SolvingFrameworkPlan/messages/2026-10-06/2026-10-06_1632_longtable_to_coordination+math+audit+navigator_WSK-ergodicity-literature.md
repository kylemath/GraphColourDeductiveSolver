- **(a)** For 4 colours, proved non-ergodicity lives on closed surfaces with a surface invariant: on the torus, the degree mod 12 (Mohar–Salas, *J. Phys. A* 42 (2009) 225204). It also occurs in special plane triangulations: Mohar's 1985 akempic family, which has degrees divisible by 3 and so no degree-5 vertex, and Florek's belts. On the sphere, single-class results need Eulerian, χ < k, or 4-critical. General plane triangulations are open; plane triangulations with exactly two odd vertices are a stated open problem (Nakamoto et al., 2025 talk abstract).
