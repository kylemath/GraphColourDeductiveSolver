# Independent structural work: rooted symmetries and vacancy progress

4 October 2026. Private math-team working note; no delegation or reply to other teams.

## A precise symmetry bound

For a connected oriented map with a single cyclic rotation at each vertex, a rotation-preserving automorphism is determined by the image of one dart. To prove this, if it fixes a dart (u,v), rotation preservation forces it to fix every neighbour of u, and the reverse dart (v,u). Repeat across connected edges. Every vertex is reached, so the automorphism is identity. The same uniqueness holds between two globally orientation-reversing automorphisms, since their composite preserves orientation.

Consequently the subgroup fixing a root r has at most 2 deg(r) elements when every allowed automorphism preserves all rotations or reverses them consistently. At a degree-five root the bound is ten. No claim about arbitrary graph automorphism groups follows without connecting them to the map rotations. Isolated vertices and disconnected components invalidate the propagation argument.

This supports a warning charging bound ONLY if the fibre of each charge label is contained in one actual rooted-map symmetry orbit. A boundary pattern modulo symmetry does not imply that condition: unseen interior colourings can have the same pattern and fail to be symmetric. For a finite charge set L, a proved per-label orbit condition yields at most 10 |L| distinct warned colourings, even if each label's representative orbit differs. The required orbit-fibre condition is the missing mathematics, not the group bound. Rooted-map symmetries cannot justify an assumed bounded multiplicity of interior switches.

## Why changing the vacant root is a substantive alternative

A singleton neighbour's colour can fill the vacancy while that neighbour becomes vacant. This moves between proper deletion colourings coherently and does not combine unrelated counterexample witnesses. The operation is reversible, so termination requires a single potential on (vacant root, partial colouring), fixed before the run. A potential only on the original boundary cannot automatically transport through the slide.

At order 17 graph 0 root 4 state 25, every directly available singleton slide moves the vacancy to a degree-six vertex. A useful induction-compatible theory must therefore permit higher-degree vacancies temporarily, or prove a specific preceding Kempe preparation. The degree-five-root hypothesis cannot simply be applied after each slide.

A minimal future candidate should name: admissible partial states; the set of permissible vacancies; legal Kempe moves and singleton slides; a graph-defined fixed rank; and target as a free colour at the vacancy. Every component swap remains relative to the current deletion graph. The existence of some extendible future state is not an invariant.
