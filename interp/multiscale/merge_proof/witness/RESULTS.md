## 1. τ*: definition and what it changes

**Definition [P].** For an even pocket walk (H_0 … H_m, H_m = H_0 + t), τ_j is the one-lap circumradius of the lap started at boundary vertex b_j, and **τ\* = min_j τ_j** = min over windows of m+1 consecutive points of the periodic lift. For an odd pocket τ is already base-point free (the doubled walk is centrally symmetric about q), so τ\* = τ. Still τ\* ≥ |t|/2. I did **not** take the cylinder radius in R²/⟨t⟩: a lap 0, t, 2t, …, 50t, … back to t has cylinder radius 0 and is maximally tilted, so that quotient hides drift along t; "min over base points" is the clean choice. SAT: the re-level is run once per base point (ranked by τ_j of the current colouring, triangle pockets fixed to one base point), min over base points; all m base points were tried in `t1` (≤ 220 calls per merge, ≤ 16 s).

**Answer to Task 1 [M].** For even fused pockets the w ≥ 4 effect is entirely the base-point artefact: w\* ≤ 2 in 27/28, ≤ 3 in all. For odd fused pockets (3 vortices; 13 of the 14 w ≥ 4 merges regenerated here, and most of the 68 in `../RESULTS.md`) τ is base-point free and **w = 4–5 is real**. So the 75% conjecture of `../RESULTS.md` §3 was half right: it explains the even cases only. The odd cases are not Lemma M either (B odd ⇒ some renaming always admits |t| ≤ 2); `t1b` shows the sub-disc rings at depth 1–5 inside the fixed interior have τ\* = 2.6–3.6, i.e. the collar must interpolate from a tilted fixed ring to a level boundary around a centre pinned by the vortex heights. [C, 70%] w for odd fusions is bounded by (interior ring tilt) + O(1) and cannot be bounded by a boundary-only invariant; the invariant should bound the tilt of the ring at depth 1–2 as well (a "thick boundary" condition), or accept w ~ 5.

## 2. Lemma M witness: built, (a_W) as stated is refuted [M] + [P]

Construction (`t2.py`): C1 = ball(mid of a vortex pair, 5+k) on ico10 (exactly 2 vortices); on ∂C1 pick an edge uw whose outer apex x has only u, w as C1-neighbours; D2 = component of (ball(x, r2) minus the 1-shell of C1∖{u,w}, minus a bisector room around u's far fan vertex) containing x, with exactly 2 vortices; A = C1 ∪ D2 touches C1 only along uw, so uw is a chord separating two two-vortex pockets (the half-ball D2 is jagged and adds triangle pockets, which carry t = 0). B = the component of ball(u, 3)∖A on u's side, vortex-free (9 vertices); in U = A ∪ B the vertex u is interior, the chord disappears, and the pocket of U containing C1 also contains D2's big pocket (8 pockets of U in all). A is coloured by `mp.sat2` with every pocket τ ≤ 1 and the big-walk constraint |t_A|² ≥ 12 (SAT in 10 s), B by `level_cols`.

- (i) [M] For all 24 renamings the fused pocket has |t_P| = |t_A| = 2√3 after the min-R repair whenever the repair zone is vortex-free (23/24). The one exception is the Lemma's own caveat: an R = 1 zone that swallowed a depth-1 vortex, which changed t_P to 0. Hence τ\*(P) ≥ √3 > 1 for every renaming and every recolouring that avoids the vortices [P from §1.1 of `../RESULTS.md`]: **no vortex-free (R, w) exists, (a_W) as stated is false**, and w ≥ depth of the nearest vortex of A from ∂U [P]. (ii) [M, weak] only depth-1/2 instances finished: w\* = 2–3 with min depth 1 (k = 1) and 2 (k = 2 run). ## 3. W2, precisely, and whether it is preserved

**W2 [definition].** c on U is W2-level if (a) τ\*(P, c) ≤ 1 for every pocket P, and (b) for every connected sub-union Q of pockets (subtree of T(U)) with an even number of interior odd vertices, |t_Q| ≤ 2, t_Q the translation monodromy of ∂Q. (b) for Q = U is the strict |t_U| ≤ 2; (a) implies (b) for single pockets; odd Q need no condition (their fusion with an odd Q′ is an even sub-union, covered). Finite-state: still a property of c|∂U read along T(U).

- Satisfiable at leaves [M]: every node in the ico10 run and 227/231 in flip10_1 satisfy (b) already; (a) is the leaf SAT. Adding (b) to `wk.sat` is a big-walk constraint per sub-union (|H_m − H_0|² ≤ 4, as in `mp.sat2`), not implemented here.
- Not automatically preserved [M]: 4/231 nodes in the flip10_1 L = 12 recursion violate (b) with |t_Q| = 2√3 while every pocket is level; their parents still merged with w ≤ 1, because at 14–36 vertices every vortex is within reach of the collar. So W1's measured w ≤ 3 in `../../hierarchy/weak` survived (b)-violations only by vortex proximity [C, 80%].
- Is W2 enough for (a_W)? [P, negative example on paper] Two fused pockets P_1, P_2 along one seam with (a_1, b_1) = (2e, 2e′) and (a_2, b_2) = (2e, −2e′): one D6 renaming cannot make both |a + P b| ≤ 2 (angle to a and to −a both ≥ 120° is impossible), so a single-renaming merge of W2 sides can still force a collar to a vortex. [C, 60%] the right condition is on the pair (A, B) along the seam, not on each side: the seam renaming must be allowed to vary pocket by pocket, or the invariant must bound the ring tilt at depth 1–2 (§1), which is also what the odd w = 4 instances ask for.

## 4. Reviewer's intrinsic τ (strip half-width), `w2.tau_strip` [M, partial]

Definition implemented: odd → max |H_i − q| (same as before); even, t ≠ 0 → half-width of the narrowest strip parallel to t containing the periodic lift (= half the range of the component of H_i perpendicular to t); t = 0 → circumradius. τ_strip ≤ τ\* ≤ τ_onelap [P], so (a) **T = 1 is satisfiable at every leaf and pocket already** (all 2,129 leaves and every re-levelled node have τ_onelap ≤ 1); measured on 405 nodes of three runs (`w2check_summary.jsonl`): τ_strip ∈ {0.25, 0.43, 0.5, 0.87, 1.0}, never above 1; τ_onelap = 1 at 401 of them. No node needs T > 1. (b) The exact min w under τ_strip ≤ 1 needs a SAT encoding with a free strip direction per pocket (not written: selector variables over t ∈ 2Λ, |t| ≤ 4, and strip offsets); what is available is the sandwich w_strip ≤ w\* ≤ w, so for even fusions w_strip ≤ 3 (≤ 2 in 27/28), and for odd fusions **all three definitions coincide, so w = 4–5 stands under the reviewer's τ too** (§1). Caveat [P]: τ_strip does not bound |t| (a lap hugging a line can carry any t), so W2 must keep |t_Q| ≤ 2 explicitly; τ\* has it built in. Closings at several seam lengths were not re-run in this time box (`../../closing/` has them under the old τ).

## 5. c\* test (reviewer's comb) [M on m = 5, 10, 20, 30; P for the mechanism]

`cstar.py`: triangular-lattice patch plus an apex (a triangulation), c\* the 4-row periodic colouring. Checks: (i) c\* is proper, every vertex even (w = 0), every horizontal edge labelled a; its height is H = (x − y/2)e_a + O(1): slope 1 along x, constant along (1, 2) [P, by the checkerboard sign rule]. A = the seam row 0 plus row −1 (teeth of depth 1), chords (2k, 0)(2k, −1): all 3m − 3 pockets are triangles, τ = τ\* = τ_strip = 0.87, strict τ(∂A) = 7.3, 14.8, 29.8, 44.8 for m = 5, 10, 20, 30, t = 0. (ii) **Teeth deeper than 1 row are not level**: with depth-2 teeth the non-triangle pockets have τ = 7.0 (m = 5) under every definition, because c\* drifts along the tooth boundary at slope 1; a width-2 tooth that stays level must run along (1, 2), and then it has no interior beyond distance 1 from ∂A. (iii) Merge with a flat level B (SAT, rectangle above the seam): m = 5, 10, 20, 30 → **R = 1, w = 0 for every m**: the seam conflicts are dense (2.6 per m) and N_1(conflicts) ⊇ A, so the repair alone recolours the whole comb and the fused pocket is level with no collar. [P] This is general: weak 1-levelness forces c\*-coloured pockets to be within distance 1 of ∂A, so A ⊂ N_1(∂U) and the collar/zone can rewrite it; the drift 2m·e_a of the seam costs nothing in (R, w). What it does break is the Mismatch Lemma W bound (linear in crossed pockets) as a *proof* tool, not the measured constants. (iv) W2(b) (sub-union monodromy) does **not** exclude c\*: t_Q = 0 for every sub-union (no vortices). A τ-version does: the union of two adjacent teeth has τ ≥ 2 (depth 1: strict τ of the whole comb grows like m/2), so "τ\* ≤ 1 on every pocket and ≤ 2 on every union of two adjacent pockets" excludes c\* for m ≥ 3, and a bound S_0 on seam-crossed pockets excludes it for m > S_0 only. Since (iii) shows c\* is harmless for (R, w), the τ-on-unions condition is not needed for the constants; it is needed only if one wants Lemma W to give a bound.
